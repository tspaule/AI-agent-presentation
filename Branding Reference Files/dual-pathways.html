Two Ways to Respond

Client asks

"Should I switch from sole trader to a company structure?"

Technical Answer
Obvious
  • Company tax rate is 25%
  • Setup costs around $1,500
  • Annual ASIC fees apply
  • Directors have legal obligations
  • Limited liability protection
Questions That Matter
Non-obvious
  • What's prompting this question now?
  • Tax, liability, or something else?
  • Planning to bring on investors?
  • What does the business look like in 3 years?
  • Does this structure still fit then?

One path gives information. The other creates clarity.
