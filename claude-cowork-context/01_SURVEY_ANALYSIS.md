# Survey Analysis — AI Agent Session Intake Form

## Response Summary
**Total Responses:** 12
**Collection Period:** Feb 6-8, 2026

---

## Q1: Experience Level with AI Tools

| Level | Count | Percentage |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Daily User (Integral to workflow) | 5 | 42% |
| Occasional User (Few times a week/month) | 7 | 58% |
| Beginner/Curious | 0 | 0% |

**Insight:** Audience is split — nearly half are power users, half are still exploring. Content needs to serve both.

---

## Q2: What Comes to Mind When You Hear "AI Agents"?

**Themes:**
- "Bots that complete repetitive tasks, help with research"
- "The autonomous assistance I know is out there" (gap between current tools and potential)
- "Chat GPT" (many conflate agents with chatbots)
- "Help" / "Time and cost efficiency"
- "Untrustworthy. Political tools to gather information." (skepticism)
- "I need this for daily/weekly workflow"
- Limited understanding of actual use cases

**Insight:** Mixed understanding. Some see the potential, others conflate agents with chatbots, one is skeptical. Opportunity to clarify the distinction.

---

## Q3: What Confuses You Most About AI Agents?

**Top Confusions:**
1. **How to build one in a secure environment** (security focus)
2. **Too many tools — which one to use?** (choice paralysis)
3. **Can they work across different apps?** (integration questions)
4. **How to use them effectively and efficiently**
5. **Setup and control**
6. **Best practice**

**Key Quotes:**
- "How to actually use them. How to set up an environment that enables me to plug them in across my data in a safe way."
- "The amount of options out there and how best to harness the power of it"

**Insight:** Confusion centers on practical implementation — security, tool selection, and cross-app integration.

---

## Q4: Tedious Tasks That Should Be Easier

**Ranked by Frequency:**
1. **Email management** — 4 mentions
   - "Staying on top of emails"
   - "Email management"
   - "Planning and sorting emails"
   
2. **Data reporting & analysis** — 2 mentions
   - "Weekly reporting data insights from trading week across two data sites (Quantium - Woolworths) and (Circana - Coles). The process takes a few hours to download, direct, report and publish."
   
3. **Context loading into AI** — 2 mentions
   - "Copying and pasting everything into gpt"
   - "I'm starting to find it very tedious pushing context into ChatGPT... I really wish that I could automatically connect it to more sources"

4. **Client monitoring & touchpoints** — 1 mention
   - "Monitoring clients and periodic touch points (emails calls messages)"

5. **Proposals & scoping** — 1 mention
   - "Scoping and sending out proposals"

6. **Design documentation** — 1 mention
   - "Property acquisitions and the time and cost it takes to prepare design documentation"

7. **Family/work scheduling** — 1 mention
   - "Balancing school/family requirements and work hours"

8. **Case notes & invoices** — 1 mention
   - "Case notes, invoices, planning... Searching peer reviewed papers"

**Standout Quote:**
> "I've been wanting to learn how to setup an agent so that every Thursday when data is released, I come to work and a report is sitting there for me with all data analysed and ready for me to review and publish."

**Insight:** Email is the universal pain point. Data/reporting automation is a huge opportunity. The "context loading" fatigue is real — people want AI that just *knows* their situation.

---

## Q5: Biggest Concerns/Hesitations

**Ranked by Frequency:**
1. **Data Privacy and Security** — 7 mentions (58%)
2. **Accuracy and Reliability (Hallucinations)** — 6 mentions (50%)
3. **Not knowing where to start or which tool to use** — 6 mentions (50%)
4. **Cost or Required Subscription Fees** — 4 mentions (33%)

**Insight:** Privacy/security is the #1 concern — MUST address this directly and honestly. Hallucination anxiety is real. Tool overwhelm is a major barrier.

---

## Q6: One Thing to Walk Away With

**Themes:**
1. **Practical setup guidance**
   - "How to build an app using Claude code"
   - "How to build an agent in a secure environment"
   - "Practical steps to set up a usable AI stack"
   - "How to setup agents and what they can help problem solve"

2. **Tool recommendations**
   - "If there is a genuine AI agent I should be using today"
   - "Best AI Agents to use to optimise my life"
   - "Best tools and best way to use these tools"

3. **Efficiency gains**
   - "I want to be more efficient and informed"
   - "Adopting AI more regularly in my working life"

4. **Security understanding**
   - "Greater understanding on privacy/security and a more open mind"

**Insight:** People want actionable guidance — not just concepts. "What should I actually use?" is the burning question.

## Q7: Session Preference (Theory vs Practice)

| Preference | Count | Percentage |
|------------|-------|------------|
| Balanced mix of both | 10 | 83% |
| Hands-on practical demos | 2 | 17% |
| Theory/mechanics focus | 0 | 0% |

**Insight:** Nobody wants theory-only. Keep theory tight, maximize practical demos.

---

## Q8: "Dumb Questions" They've Wanted to Ask

**Notable Questions:**
1. "How much of my life does it know about me — like personal information (eg — financial, porn etc)"
2. "What happens to secondary and tertiary education if information becomes so available. How is knowledge affected as a commodity or skillset"
3. "I feel like it's just going to cost money, like I'll get hooked and have to pay for subscriptions"

**Insight:** The "how much does AI know about me" question is gold — address it directly. The education/knowledge question is philosophical but worth a quick comment. The subscription concern is practical and valid.

---

## Key Takeaways for Presentation Design

### Must Address:
- ✅ Privacy and security (head-on, honest)
- ✅ Hallucination reality and how to manage it
- ✅ Clear tool recommendations (not just "it depends")
- ✅ "How much does AI know about me?" question

### Must Demo:
- ✅ Email-related automation (universal pain)
- ✅ Data/reporting automation (high value)
- ✅ The "context loading" problem being solved

### Must Avoid:
- ❌ Too much theory without practice
- ❌ Tool overwhelm (keep recommendations tight)
- ❌ Glossing over security concerns

### Opportunity:
- The "weekly Thursday report waiting for me" use case is a perfect demo scenario
- Show the evolution from "copying into GPT" to "agent that already knows"
